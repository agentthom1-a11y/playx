# PlayX product and go-to-market brief

## Positioning

**PlayX helps creators turn two songs into a deliberate DJ transition without learning advanced DAW automation.** It is not competing with the full hardware/controller ecosystem of Rekordbox or with the live-production maturity of Mixxx. It is a *transition-making studio* with an optional live Mixxx bridge.

**Ideal initial users:** beginner DJs, short-form remix editors, baile/club producers, dance studios, and content creators who have rights to the music they mix.

**Job to be done:** get a smooth, editable transition sample or an audition-ready mix within a few steps. Real pain points: finding matching tempos, arranging phrases, choosing effects, avoiding harsh audio seams, and exporting an easy-to-review file.

## Launch hypothesis (not market research facts)

1. **Free/open core:** two-track local studio, technical QA, basic transitions and public source code under MIT. Grow adoption with educational demo videos and examples made from royalty-free/licensed tracks.
2. **Pro value, if validated:** stem-aware bass swaps, transition timeline editing, LUFS/true-peak mastering, batch setlists, non-destructive projects, intelligent music discovery from local libraries, and unlimited presets. Charge for *proprietary extra services or licensed premium content* only after legal review of dependency and distribution rights; open-source rights remain intact.
3. **Creator channel:** short before/after transition clips, GitHub releases, YouTube Mixxx integrations, TikTok creator tutorials, and music-production communities. Never use unlicensed copyrighted audio for public marketing demonstrations.

## Product analytics (opt-in only, not currently implemented)

Track discover → install → first import → first successful export → first replay/share. Measure time-to-first-mix, import failure, job failure, 7-day retention, recommendation acceptance, and paid conversion where applicable.

## Scalability route

- **Phase 1: Windows desktop beta.** Offline-first, one local renderer, no server hosting bills. Fix installation friction, real-world audio quality, and trust/security.
- **Phase 2: Paid creative workflows.** Projects, timeline, auditable edits, multi-track single-pass renderer, stems and advanced recipes with deterministic execution, user consent and data controls.
- **Phase 3: Creator collaboration/business.** Optional authenticated cloud service for metadata/project synchronization, opt-in collaboration, organization billing, and proper content licensing—separate backend and privacy model. Do not expose the local app directly to the Internet.

## Key differentiation and defensibility

Transparent AI direction separated from deterministic DSP, local privacy, remix genre-specific recipes, open Mixxx interoperability, downloadable QA, and measured reproducibility. **No generative music or copyrighted samples bundled.** We will not market simple crossfades as fully autonomous production.

## Risks

Desktop setup friction, CPU-heavy phase vocoder, false BPM/key estimates, Mixxx MIDI timing drift, unpredictable effect quality, user rights to remix music, customer expectations for mastering, open-source competition, code-signing/security warnings, and acquisition costs.
